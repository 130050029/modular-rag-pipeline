Pour-Over Coffee Brewing

Pour-over coffee brewing involves slowly pouring hot water over ground coffee beans held in a filter, allowing water to extract flavor as it drips through into a carafe below. The technique gives the brewer more control over extraction time and water temperature compared to automatic drip machines.

Most guides recommend water heated to between 195 and 205 degrees Fahrenheit, and a grind consistency similar to coarse sea salt for a standard pour-over cone.
