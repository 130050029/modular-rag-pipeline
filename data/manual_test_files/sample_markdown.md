# Solar Panel Efficiency

Modern solar panels convert roughly twenty to twenty-two percent of incoming sunlight into usable electricity, though laboratory prototypes have exceeded forty percent under controlled conditions.

## Factors affecting efficiency

- Panel temperature: efficiency drops as panels heat up in direct sun
- Angle of installation relative to the sun's path
- Dust, shading, and general panel cleanliness
- Age of the panel, since output degrades roughly half a percent per year

Manufacturers typically guarantee at least eighty percent of original output after twenty-five years of use.
